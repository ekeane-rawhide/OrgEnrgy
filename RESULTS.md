## Never tested against physical reality

Every law here is a property of a model I wrote, confirmed by simulating
that same model. **Nothing in this repository has touched a physical
system, or any data not generated by its own equations.** The γ estimator
was the one instrument that could have changed that; it works on clean
synthetic trajectories and fails under 5% noise.

## The finding that actually replicates

Eight instances, across every kind of work attempted:

1. H1's fan-out test hard-coded `True`
2. H2's impossibility search scoped to always confirm
3. T4's P1 — taper inert across the entire kill window
4. H3's strawman baseline
5. H4's C2 — diffusion at the zero-diffusion limit
6. H5 — never swept its own sweep
7. L7 — read machine-precision agreement as strong evidence
8. Step 3 — reused the exact strawman-baseline error I had already
   documented one tier away

**In eight of eight cases I did not detect the error from the inside, and
in eight of eight an adversary did.** I wrote an explicit anti-vacuity
warning at the top of T4 and committed the error in the same file. Care
did not help. Pre-registration did not help — the pre-registered numbers
were themselves guesses, and three separate thresholds were tighter or
more arbitrary than the measurements could support.

What did help, every single time, was handing the work to something with
no stake in it and instructions to destroy it.

The one claim in this document supported by a large, consistent,
adversarially-tested sample is that claim. It is a fact about the
process, not about flow networks — which is not what anyone set out to
find, and is the only thing here that earned its place.
